Notes

One card shows a salary, four do not

That is the opt-in working, not an inconsistency. Salary travels from the requisition to the posting but is private unless the recruiter deliberately publishes it. A layout that reserved a salary slot on every card would make the four blanks look like missing data and pressure recruiters into filling them.

Mixed-language listings are the normal case

A teller role is written in Burmese and an engineering role in English, because that is who reads each one. The header switch changes the page's own furniture; it does not translate a posting the recruiter wrote. Pretending otherwise would need machine translation of job adverts, which is a promise nobody should make.

No account, no saved jobs, no alerts

Every one of those needs a candidate login, which needs password reset, session handling and a second identity system. Nothing in Module 2 asks for it. The link is the account: it is shareable, resumable and costs nothing to maintain.

Two filters, not seven

Five open roles do not need faceted search. Location and team are the two axes a candidate actually thinks in; an experience-level or salary-band filter over five results is furniture that costs a mobile data round trip.
