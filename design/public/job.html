About the role

You will visit customers across Yangon to agree repayment plans, working alongside branch teams and the credit risk unit. Most of your week is spent out of the office, and you will plan your own route.

What you will do

  • Visit 8–12 customers a day across your assigned townships
  • Agree repayment plans and record them the same day
  • Escalate cases that need restructuring to the credit risk unit

What we need from you

  • Two years in collections, field sales or a similar visiting role
  • A valid motorbike licence
  • Comfortable writing short notes in Burmese and reading English forms
