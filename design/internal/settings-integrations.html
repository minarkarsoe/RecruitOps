Email & calendar

Module 3 cannot send an interview invitation and Module 4 cannot send an offer without one of these. Everything below them in the product waits on this row.

Not connected

Mail only, no calendar. The fallback for an air-gapped install with its own mail relay — which is a real customer shape under ADR-0004, so it is a first-class option here rather than a hidden advanced setting.

HRMS & payroll

Module 4 ends when a candidate accepts. The employee record starts somewhere else, and this is the seam. The sales requirement names four systems in this market.

QHRM

Not connected

Also listed by sales: BetterHR, GlobalTA, CityHR.

Write-only. Once saved it is never displayed again, not even to an admin — a field that can show a secret back is a field that can leak one over a shoulder or into a screenshot. Replacing it is the only edit.

Where this value is stored is an open decision

Module 7 says credentials never sit in the database in plaintext, and lists key vault versus encrypted column as needing an ADR. The two answers behave differently on this screen: a vault install would show a vault reference here instead of a text field, and an offline install may have no vault at all. Do not build this field until that is decided.

One question sales has not answered

On “hired”, is the employee record created in the HRMS automatically? If yes, this integration writes to the customer's payroll system of record, and a mis-mapped field becomes someone's wrong salary. That deserves a dry-run mode and a preview of the payload before the first live write — neither of which is drawn, because the direction is not settled.

Data retention & recovery

Per-company, in line with that company's SLA. This is the section that deletes things, so it is the one drawn most carefully.

Retention policy

Records older than the period are purged on a schedule. Purge is permanent.

Record type Keep for Then Affected today
Rejected candidates Profile, CV file, extracted text 1,204
Interview feedback Scorecards and debrief notes Delete 0
Approval records Who approved what, and when Forever — —
Audit log Sign-ins, permission changes, exports Forever — —

Two rows have no control, and that is the design. Approval records and the audit log are the product's evidence that a decision happened. A configurable retention on them would make the guarantee conditional on a dropdown, so they are stated as permanent rather than offered as a choice.

Apply retention change?

Shortening rejected-candidate retention from 5 years to 2 years will permanently remove:

  • 1,204candidate profiles
  • 1,188CV files
  • 96of them applied to more than one job

Stage history is kept — Module 5's charts are built on it and cannot be rebuilt afterwards.

The system does not know your legal minimum

Module 7 asks who is accountable when a company sets a period shorter than the law allows. The honest answer a screen can give is that the software has no view: it enforces the number it is given. So the confirmation counts real rows and makes the admin type the count, rather than implying a validated “safe” range that does not exist.

Open channels

Module 8 requires a publicly reachable webhook endpoint. This is an on-premise deployment behind a corporate firewall, so the platforms cannot call in. That is a deployment fact, and the channels screen states it there rather than letting an admin connect a bot that will never receive a message.
