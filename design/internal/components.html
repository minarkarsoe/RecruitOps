What the collapsed state must keep

  • 64px, not narrower. mx-2 leaves a 48×36 target — above the 24×24 WCAG 2.5.8 AA minimum with room to spare.
  • A name on every icon. aria-label for assistive tech, title for the mouse. An icon-only rail with neither is a memory test.
  • Group rules, not group words. The headings are the one thing that genuinely cannot survive; a hairline keeps the grouping they carried.
  • The toggle does not move. Same slot in both states, in the pinned footer.
  • The state persists. A width preference that resets every visit is not a preference.

The accordion question is deliberately not answered here. At 11 links under 4 headings the flat list fits one viewport; revisit past roughly 18–20.

Empty, error and overlay states

An empty state teaches the interface rather than announcing absence. An error names the recovery. A modal is a last resort — inline and progressive alternatives come first.
