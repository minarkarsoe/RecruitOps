How an interviewer ends up with this list

Posting override Technology Company-wide

Drawn as a resolved path rather than described in a sentence. Three levels are easy to write down and hard to hold in your head, and getting it wrong means an interviewer is scored against criteria nobody expected — which nobody notices until the debrief.

Criteria

Order is list position — saved without gaps

  • Guidance — what the numbers mean

  • A rating with no guidance is two managers scoring different things and calling it one number.

  • Yes / no does not enter the average — it is a gate, not a grade.

  • Required text is the field that makes a 2 defensible when the candidate asks why.

  • Three types, deliberately.

What the interviewer will see

System design

Required

5 = designed a system this size before and can defend the trade-offs. 3 = follows a known pattern well. 1 = cannot describe the trade-offs.

Holds a valid work permit

Required

Evidence for your scores

Required

Detented rating buttons, not a slider. A 1–5 judgement has five legal answers and a slider invites a 3.5 that the schema will reject — the control should not be able to express a value the system cannot store.

This screen does not control blindness

Whether a panellist sees other people's scores before submitting is a rule about the interview, not about the template — so there is no “blind” switch here. Putting one on a per-template basis would let a department turn the product's own guarantee off by editing a form.

Editing a live template does not rewrite history

Submitted responses keep the criterion they were answered against, so removing “Code quality” today does not blank it on 34 interviews from last quarter. The template deactivates rather than deletes for the same reason — the sidebar keeps the 2025 version because interviews still point at it.

A department with no template

Collections uses the company-wide default

That is a working state, not a gap — five general criteria are already being used on every Collections interview. Give it its own list only when the general ones stop making two candidates comparable.

“Start from the default” is the primary action. An empty list is how a department ends up with three criteria that overlap the general five and comparison quietly dies.
