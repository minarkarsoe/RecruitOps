Why this is scheduled rather than immediate

A candidate who accepts in August and never arrives in October is a real outcome. Writing them into payroll six weeks early means someone has to delete an employee record from the company's system of record — a worse job than creating one late.

One export contract, not four integrations

Sales named QHRM, BetterHR, GlobalTA and CityHR. Building four vendor connectors into core would make every one of them a permanent maintenance cost. ADR-0007's extension point is the shape: one documented payload, adapters outside the core.

These files are the reason the retention screen exists

An NRC scan and a bank account number have no reason to sit in a recruitment database once they have reached the HRMS. The retention policy in Integrations & data policy governs them, and this is the screen that should link to it — a company that never opens that page will keep every NRC it has ever collected, indefinitely, without deciding to.
