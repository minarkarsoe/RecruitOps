Requisition & headcount governance

The chain is copied onto the requisition the moment it is submitted, so editing the template later cannot rewrite a decision already made. Budget and headcount thresholds extend the chain on their own. A rejection opens a new round beside the old one, never over it.

Draft PendingApproval Approved / Rejected

A senior approver can close every step at or below their own position in one action, but may only reject their own. The record names who actually acted, not who the template expected.

Search that works in Burmese

Burmese does not put spaces between words, so ordinary full-text search silently returns nothing. CV search is built on trigram indexing instead, which does not need word boundaries to exist.

Questions procurement always asks

If yours isn't here, ask it in the pilot conversation.

Where does our data physically live?

In a database that belongs to your deployment alone. We install one application instance and one database per company: either in a cloud account we operate for you, or inside your own infrastructure on Enterprise. Nothing is pooled with another customer at any tier.

Can we change the approval chain ourselves?

Yes. Chains are configuration, built in the interface, per department. Editing one never disturbs a requisition already in flight: the chain is copied onto the requisition at submission, so decisions already recorded cannot be altered by a later template change.

How does search handle Burmese?

Two problems, handled separately. Encoding: Zawgyi and Unicode occupy the same code block, so Zawgyi text looks valid and never matches. Every entry point converts to Unicode before storage and keeps the original for diagnosis. Tokenisation: Burmese has no reliable word spacing, so search uses trigram indexing rather than word-boundary full-text search.

Can a hiring manager see another department's candidates?

No. Access is scoped to the departments they own, and an out-of-scope record returns 404 rather than 403, so they cannot even confirm it exists. The one deliberate exception is panel membership, which grants read access to exactly one application, which is what makes cross-department interview panels possible.

What happens when a candidate asks to be deleted?

An erasure workflow removes their personal data and attached documents while preserving the anonymised hiring statistics your reporting depends on. Candidate records also age out under a two-year retention policy. These are capabilities to support your PDPA and GDPR obligations. We do not hold a compliance certification, and we will not claim one.

Do we get a customised version?

You get a configured one, which is better. Approval chains, scorecard criteria, application form fields, JD templates and roles are all data in your own database. We deliberately never fork the codebase per customer. A private branch is what stops you receiving the fixes and upgrades everyone else gets.
